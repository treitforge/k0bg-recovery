Amateur Radio Sites

AC6V Have a question about any facet of amateur radio? Here's the first place to look.

ARRL American Radio and Relay League is our political voice. Every amateur should belong.

BAMA Own a boat anchor and need a manual? This is the place!

cebik.com L. B. Cebik's, W4RNL (sk), antenna site for the technically inspired, and registration is free.

CQ Ham This Russian site has a ton of owners and repair manuals.

eham.net The best "elmer" site on the internet.

EZNEC This is where to download Roy Lewallen's, W7EL, famous software.

Electronic Labs Looking for an amateur radio or electronics project? This is the place!

Famous Amateurs Have you worked any of these famous people?

KB2LJJ If you need a manual or schematic, this is the first place to try. Through the efforts of Joao Ribeiro, the material is free, but if you visit often, send some money his way.

K6MHE Danny Richardson's VHF mobile antenna modeling article (in pdf) is a must read especially for those folks into playing the VHF Gain Game.

KBØASQ One of the better general interest personal web site.

KA6WKE, Mark Schoonover Great Mobile related blog.

LID List If you see your call and/or name here, shame on you!

NK7Z Dave Cole's equipment review site. Includes propagation forecasts.

NØHR Looking for PDA logging software, or detailed information on every facet of amateur radio? Here it is!

N4KC If you're a reader, and into amateur radio fiction, this is the site!

N6FN Bernie Lafreniere's Nifty Accessories publishes Mini-Manuals for just about every mobile radio there is, along with a bunch of, well, nifty accessories.

N9ZIA, Joe Loritz On-line analysis and conversion software for every application.

Old QSL Cards If you're a nostalgia buff, and like old QSL cards, this is THE place!

PA3DUV, Dick Knol One of the most powerful mobile installations you'll ever see.

QRZ This is a first-class amateur site and has one of the largest international call sign databases available anywhere.

Radioing If you want a straight-forward explanation of RFI, EMI, EMC, EME or a host of other amateur radio-related jargon, this is the site.

Repeater Builder Even if you're not into building FM repeaters, this site has a host of information pertinent to any VHF operation.

Thiecom This is a German site, but has a listing for user and maintenance manuals on a variety of amateur equipment. Look for FTP archive in left frame.

Vintage Manuals If you need a vintage manual and can't find it anywhere else, look here. Prices vary from $7 up.

VE3EDY Interesting variation of the screwdriver antenna. For more information, send Larry Parker an e-mail.

VE6AB, Jerry Clement Learn how the Canadians do it.

WØUN, John Brosnahan A DX and contesters dream.

W1AW, ARRL, Hiram Percy Maxim Memorial Station

W8JI Tom Rauch's site contains a host of technical information of interest to all amateurs.

Yaesu FT857 Users group.

3955 Group.
